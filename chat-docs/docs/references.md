# References: every source the navigator uses

All the papers, reports and datasets behind the app, by topic. The
[datasets page](datasets.md) has download links for each data file.

## Sun, Earth and the Moon's orientation

- **JPL DE421 ephemeris** and the DE421 lunar orientation files (NASA NAIF):
  where the Sun, Earth and Moon are, and how the Moon is turned.
- **JPL Horizons:** used to check the app's Sun and Earth positions (they
  match to about 0.00001°).
- **Kopp & Lean 2011**, Geophysical Research Letters: the solar constant,
  1361 W/m².

## Terrain and sunlight

- **Barker et al. 2023**, Planetary Science Journal
  ([doi:10.3847/PSJ/acf3e1](https://doi.org/10.3847/PSJ/acf3e1)): the LOLA
  south-pole elevation maps (20, 80 and 240 m), NASA PGDA product 90.
- **Barker et al. 2021**, Planetary and Space Science
  ([doi:10.1016/j.pss.2020.105119](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.pss.2020.105119)):
  sunlight 1 m and 5 m above the ground at a few south-pole sites.
- **Mazarico et al. 2011**, Icarus 211, 1066–1081
  ([doi:10.1016/j.icarus.2010.10.030](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.icarus.2010.10.030)):
  NASA's average sunlight and Earth-visibility maps, NASA PGDA product 69.
- **LROC WAC South Pole Summer Mosaic** (Robinson et al. 2010, the LROC
  team): the imagery on the 3D surface.

## The nine regions

- **USGS**, *Down Selected Artemis III Candidate Landing Site Navigational
  Grids*
  ([ScienceBase](https://www.sciencebase.gov/catalog/item/671a6fa8d34efed5620f89f8)):
  the region outlines.
- **NASA**, Artemis III candidate landing regions announcement, October 2024.

## Power

- **NASA TM-105925** (1993), First Lunar Outpost power: the itemized load
  for a crew of 4.
- **NASA VSAT report** (NTRS 20240003162) and VSAT program targets (LSIC,
  May 2021): Sun-tracking vertical arrays on masts, and array mass per watt.
- **NASA GSFC-STD-1000:** the 25% power margin at concept stage.
- **NASA VIPER overview** (NTRS 20230011558): the small-rover add-on load.
- **NASA BVAD Rev2** and **NASA-STD-3001:** oxygen, food and drinking water
  per person (reference only).
- **Csank 2023** (NASA surface habitat): crew size.

## Communications

- **NASA DSN Handbook 810-005**, Modules 101, 104 and 301: Deep Space
  Network stations, antennas and the 10° planning limit.
- **NASA/TP-2019-220391:** reference lander radio power and data rates.
- **SSTL Lunar Pathfinder Service Guide:** ESA's relay satellite orbit and
  service.

## Landing

- **NASA HLS overview, 2025** (NTRS 20250009323): the 10° touchdown and 20°
  moonwalk slope limits.
- **NASA precision landing overview** (NTRS 20250003778): landing within
  100 m of the target.
- **NASA and Intuitive Machines releases** on IM-1 (2024) and IM-2 (2025):
  the two landers that tipped over, used to check the slope method.

## Water ice (information only)

- **LOLA permanently shadowed areas map, 20 m**, and its list of areas over
  1 km² (NASA PGDA product 90).
- **LRO Diviner polar resource products** (Paige et al.): temperatures and
  depth to stable ice.
- **Paige et al. 2010**, Science 330, 479–482: polar cold traps.
- **Colaprete et al. 2010**, Science 330, 463–468: LCROSS finds water at
  Cabeus.
- **Li et al. 2018**, PNAS 115, 8907–8912: ice exposed at the surface in
  shadowed areas.
