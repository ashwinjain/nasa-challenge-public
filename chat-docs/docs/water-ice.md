# Water ice near each region (information only)

Nothing on this page changes a calculation. The kit mass and every ranking
stay exactly as they are. It answers one question: is there a place near
each region where water ice could survive?

Ice can only last where the Sun never shines, in permanently shadowed areas,
and only where it stays cold enough: below about 110 K (−163 °C).

## Nearest permanently shadowed area to each region

Measured in a straight line from each region's reference point (its sunniest
spot) to the edge of the nearest permanently shadowed area of at least 1 km².

| Region | Shadowed area | Distance | Hottest it gets | Share cold enough for ice at the surface |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Slater Plain | 60.6 km² | 9.7 km | 77 K | 100% |
| Haworth | 3.6 km² | 9.4 km | 73 K | 76% |
| Mons Mouton | 6.3 km² | 6.0 km | 80 K | 67% |
| Mons Mouton Plateau | 6.3 km² | 12.0 km | 80 K | 67% |
| de Gerlache Rim 2 | 3.5 km² | 6.7 km | 141 K | 45% |
| Nobile Rim 1 | 1.0 km² | 5.0 km | 180 K | 29% |
| Nobile Rim 2 | 3.6 km² | 6.6 km | 235 K | 28% |
| Malapert Massif | 1.4 km² | 10.2 km | 159 K | 27% |
| Peak Near Cabeus B | 2.2 km² | 13.5 km | 221 K | 0% (ice could last about 0.24 m down) |

## What this shows

- **Every region has a shadowed area 5 to 13.5 km away.** None is within
  walking range (the app's on-foot reach is 300 m), so reaching one needs a
  rover. That is expected: the sunniest spots are on peaks, and ice is in
  the low, dark places.
- **Slater Plain's nearest shadowed area is the largest and coldest:**
  61 km², all of it cold enough for ice at the surface.
- **Mons Mouton and Haworth** also have shadowed areas that are mostly cold
  enough (67% and 76%).
- **Peak Near Cabeus B's** nearest shadowed area is too warm for ice at the
  surface, though ice could be stable a little below it.

## Limits

- "Cold enough" is not "has ice". These maps show where ice could survive.
  How much is actually there is not known.
- Shadowed areas smaller than 1 km² are left out, so there may be small cold
  spots closer to a region than the table says.
- Distances are straight lines, not drivable routes.

## Sources

- Permanently shadowed areas: NASA LOLA 20 m map (Barker et al. 2023, PGDA

- Temperatures and ice depth: LRO Diviner polar resource products (Paige et
