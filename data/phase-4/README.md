# Phase 4: Needs vs. supply

No maps here: just `assumptions.csv`, the numbers the needs and supply
models use. Each row has a central value, a low/high range, units and a
source.

Every row names its source; rows marked "Our estimate" or "Project choice"
have none and say so.

One mission: a crew of 4 landing in early 2026,
staying a year by default. The result is the **tonnage to land**: the power
kit, solar array + battery. Air, water and food are listed here for
reference but not counted (with a fixed crew and stay they weigh the same at
every site). Landers, payload limits and prices
were dropped from the rows, along with the robotic and short-crew profiles.

Default scenario: everything is brought from Earth (no water recycling, no
local ice). Local ice is never counted: Phase 7 only shows how far the nearest ice is.
